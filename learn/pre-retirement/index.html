For people designing the next chapter — lifestyle, location, healthcare, Roth timing, and legacy — with deep links into the live Retirement Lab.

Who this is for

Ages roughly 55+ (and anyone within a decade of leaving full-time work): you need more than a savings rate — you need a coherent plan for where you’ll live, how healthcare works, when to convert Roth dollars, and what “enough” looks like.

Unlike thinner stage hubs, Pre-Retirement already has a live lab under /retirement/ and /Retirement-simulator/. Standalone Learn lesson modules for this stage are still partial; use the lab pages below as the primary curriculum.

FAQ

Who is Pre-Retirement for?

Primarily ages 55+, plus anyone within about a decade of leaving full-time work who needs lifestyle, location, healthcare, and tax sequencing — not just a savings rate.

Where should I start in the Retirement Lab?

Begin at the retirement hub, then continue into the Retirement Gateway for guided onboarding.

Can I model retiring overseas?

Yes — use the overseas retirement hub to compare cities and lifestyle cost.

How is this different from Peak?

Peak (40–55) emphasizes tax coordination while still earning. Pre-Retirement emphasizes executing lifestyle, healthcare, location, and withdrawal design — with a thicker live lab already available.
