Reciprocal museum programs

A single museum membership can unlock free admission at hundreds of museums across the country — if it's the right membership. These are the reciprocal networks that turn one card into a cultural passport.

ASTC Travel Passport

Visit

The deal: One science-museum membership → free admission at 300+ science centers, planetariums, and children's museums worldwide.

Use it well: Buy the cheapest qualifying membership at your local ASTC museum, then use it on trips — not at home.

Watch out: 90-mile exclusion: no reciprocity near your home address or home museum. Call ahead — some museums cap daily reciprocal admissions.

NARM

Visit

The deal: Free admission plus shop and event discounts at 1,000+ art, history, and cultural institutions across North America.

Use it well: Usually requires the $100+ membership tier at your home museum — confirm the tier before joining.

Watch out: Some museums exclude nearby members (15-mile rule); special exhibitions are often excluded.

ROAM

Visit

The deal: Free general admission at member art and history museums across North America and beyond.

Use it well: Comes bundled with upper-tier memberships at participating museums — check your museum's tiers before upgrading.

Watch out: 25-mile local exclusion at many institutions, and benefits are set per museum.

ACM Reciprocal Network

Visit

The deal: 50% off admission for up to six people at participating children's museums — the family budget play.

Use it well: Best for families with little kids doing children's museums on road trips.

Watch out: Half off, not free — and the cardholder must be present for the discount.

Time Travelers

Visit

The deal: Free or discounted admission at 300+ history museums, historic sites, and societies across the US.

Use it well: Often bundled free with local historical-society memberships — check what you already have.

Watch out: History-only, and the benefit level varies by site.

How to stack it

  • Before any trip, check which reciprocal networks your existing memberships already include — you may be covered.
  • Join at the cheapest home museum that unlocks the network you travel with most.
  • Stack with library museum-pass programs for free local visits.
