Cheaper rental cars

Rental car prices swing wildly — the trick isn't finding the cheapest quote once, it's re-shopping it until pickup day. Book refundable early, then let automation do the rest.

AutoSlash

Visit

The deal: Free reservation tracking: hand it your refundable booking and it rebooks you automatically when the price drops.

Use it well: Book the earliest refundable rate you can find, then submit the confirmation to AutoSlash's tracker and forget about it until the alert emails arrive.

Watch out: Some agencies blocked AutoSlash's initial search display — today it's best used as a tracker for reservations made elsewhere.

Costco Travel

Visit

The deal: Consistently among the lowest member rates, with free cancellation and a free additional driver.

Use it well: Compare against your AutoSlash-tracked price — take whichever is lower at each re-check before the trip.

Watch out: Requires a Costco membership; inventory can sell out on peak weekends.

How to stack it

  • Always book the pay-later refundable rate first — never prepay a rental car.
  • Pay with a card carrying primary rental collision coverage (Ritz-Carlton, Venture X, Sapphire Reserve) and decline the counter insurance.
  • Check Costco, AAA, and airline-partner discount codes before the first booking, not after.
