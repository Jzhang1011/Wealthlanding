How to read this model

Capacity says what the org can sell; pipeline says what the funnel will deliver — triangulate, then take the lower number into the board plan. Bookings are a sales metric; revenue is an accounting outcome spread over the contract term, so term length and booking timing move revenue without moving bookings. Retention compounds: one point of gross retention is worth more than one point of attainment at Medallia's scale. Illustrative example only — not Medallia actuals, not financial advice.
