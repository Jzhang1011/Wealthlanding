Comprehensive Model Assumptions & Methodology
  • Initial Balances: Starting portfolio is $4.76M based on actual account balances from August 2026. S&P 500 funds are consolidated.
  • Wages & Savings: You and Jie both earn $350,000/yr until your respective designated retirement years. 401(k) contributions are strictly modeled at $23,500/yr each, plus an 8% employer match.
  • Passive Rental Income: Modeled as fully taxable ordinary income that adds directly to your MAGI. It continues indefinitely and inflates dynamically based on the General Inflation slider.
  • Side Hustle Income: Treated as non-taxable cash flow. It ends completely at age 60. This income directly offsets your living expenses, and any surplus automatically spills over into your Taxable Brokerage account for future use.
  • Taxes (Working vs. Retired): Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) and qualified dividends are taxed at 29.55% while working (assumes highest federal bracket + Net Investment Income Tax) and 20.75% in retirement. Ordinary income tax on non-qualified dividends is modeled at a blended 29.5%.
  • Portfolio Dividends: Taxable dividend yield is dynamically derived from August 2026 balances (~1.79%). 80% of these dividends are assumed to be "Qualified" and taxed at LTCG rates. Roth and HSA yields are fixed at 1.5%.
  • College Funding Timeline: Tuition is modeled for 3 children based on the user-selected cost per year. The twins attend from 2032–2035 and 2033–2036. The third child attends from 2036–2039. Tuition inflates at the General Inflation rate + 2.0%.
  • ACA Healthcare (Ends at Age 65): The ACA premium is strictly tied to MAGI (wages + conversions + pre-tax withdrawals + taxable dividends + capital gains + rental income). It is calculated dynamically up to the set FPL limit % and maxes out at the benchmark cost. This cost drops to $0 when you turn 65 and transition to Medicare.
  • IRMAA Medicare Surcharges (Starts Age 63): Medicare Part B and Part D premiums are subject to an income surcharge (IRMAA) based on MAGI from 2 years prior. The model strictly evaluates your MAGI starting at Age 63 and immediately accrues the exact corresponding penalty based on the 2026 MFJ tier boundaries.
  • Withdrawal Sequencing: Annual cash flow deficits (Spend + College + ACA + IRMAA) are funded in this exact order: Taxable Brokerage → Pre-Tax Accounts → Roth IRAs.
  • Roth Conversions & Inflation Adjustments: Conversions are executed annually to fill any remaining room up to the exact limit of the selected Federal Marginal Bracket. Crucially, both the Standard Deduction and all Federal Bracket Ceilings (including IRMAA tiers) are mathematically inflated each year by your selected General Inflation rate. This prevents artificial "bracket creep" from ruining the projection accuracy over 25 years.
