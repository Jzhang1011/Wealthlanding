Contact WealthLanding

Spotted an error, a broken calculator, or a rule that changed? We want to hear about it. Careful corrections from readers make every guide better.

How to reach us

Email: admin@wealthlanding.com. Write to us with a correction, a question about a tool, or a topic you would like covered.

Newsletter: you can also subscribe with the form in the site footer below to get new guides by email.

When you write about a specific page, please include the page address (URL), what looks wrong, and, if you can, the official source that shows the correct figure. That lets us check and fix it quickly.

What we can help with

  • Corrections to figures, dates, rules, or broken links
  • Calculator results that look wrong, with the inputs you used
  • Ideas for guides, checklists, or tools
  • Questions about how a tool or example works

What we cannot do

  • Give personalized investment, tax, legal, or insurance advice
  • Review your accounts, statements, or tax returns
  • Recommend a specific adviser, insurer, or product for you
  • Help with accounts at banks, brokers, airlines, or hotels

How we handle corrections

We check each reported error against the primary source (for example the IRS, the Social Security Administration, Medicare.gov, or the program's own terms). If the page is wrong, we fix it. Where a change affects the meaning of a guide or a calculator result, we say so on the page.

Read more about how pages are researched in our editorial standards and methodology, or learn who WealthLanding is for.

Need help with a decision now?

For decisions that are hard to reverse, such as claiming Social Security, choosing a Medicare plan, or converting a retirement account, talk to a licensed professional who can see your full situation. Free, unbiased Medicare counseling is available through your state's SHIP program (shiphelp.org), and the Social Security Administration answers benefit questions at ssa.gov.
