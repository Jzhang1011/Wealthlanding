World of Hyatt: elite tiers, the award chart and who it actually fits

Hyatt keeps a published award chart, a generous top tier and a Chase card with uncapped night credits, but it has fewer hotels than its rivals. Here is how status and awards work after the 2026 chart update, and when Hyatt is the right home for your nights.

Snapshot: what World of Hyatt is and how status works

World of Hyatt grades members into three elite tiers by qualifying nights or Base Points in a calendar year: Discoverist at 10 nights or 25,000 Base Points, Explorist at 30 or 50,000, and Globalist at 60 or 100,000. Lifetime Globalist comes at 1,000,000 lifetime Base Points.

Elite tiers and key benefits

Globalist is the tier people talk about. Hyatt describes Globalist stay benefits as complimentary breakfast, the best room available up to a standard suite, and late checkout at participating hotels.

Lower confidence: Hyatt’s tier pages blocked our automated checks, so this page lists only benefits we could confirm in Hyatt’s own program overview and newsroom. Discoverist and Explorist have more perks than shown here; see hyatt.com for the current list.

Milestone Rewards and Guest of Honor

According to Hyatt’s announcement, Milestone Rewards start at 20 qualifying nights (or 35,000 Base Points) and continue every 10 nights up to 150, with choices that include bonus points, suite upgrades and experience credits. From 40 nights (or 65,000 Base Points), Guest of Honor awards give Globalist benefits on eligible stays of up to seven nights, for you or someone you gift them to.

Award redemption

The short version: Hyatt still publishes an award chart with fixed point amounts, and in 2026 it added more price levels inside each category.

Hyatt says only a limited number of hotels will move a limited number of nights into Upper and Top in 2026, with broader adoption in later years. It reviews hotel categories every year and announces changes in April; bookings made before a change are honored as booked.

Award flexibility (editorial judgment): A published chart with category caps is the most predictable of the four programs, and Chase transfers make it easy to top up. The smaller footprint limits where you can use it.

How to earn status, and the cards that matter

You earn status by nights or Base Points, and the World of Hyatt Credit Card adds qualifying night credits each year and on spend. The card does not grant Explorist or Globalist on its own.

Co-brand cards

Transferable points

We found no standing, officially published status match or challenge for World of Hyatt. Hyatt also offers a loyalty collaboration with American Airlines AAdvantage; see hyatt.com for current terms.

Card status is rented, not owned. It lasts only while you keep the card, the annual fee recurs, and spending money you would not otherwise spend to reach a threshold is never worth it.

Best fit for traveler types

Hotels have no hubs, so fit depends on footprint and travel style. Before you commit, check that Hyatt has hotels you would choose anyway in the places you go most.
