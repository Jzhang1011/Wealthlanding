Hotel loyalty programs

Which hotel loyalty program fits you: Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt or IHG

The four big U.S. hotel programs reward the same thing, nights in their hotels, but they pay it back very differently. Compare what each tier actually gets you, how awards are priced, which co-brand cards hand you status, and which program suits the way you travel.

Four programs, four different bets

Marriott Bonvoy bets on size: the largest brand list, five elite tiers and points that also convert to airline miles. Hilton Honors bets on reach and easy status, with stays, nights or spend all counting and cards that hand out Gold and Diamond. World of Hyatt bets on a published award chart and a generous top tier, with a smaller footprint. IHG One Rewards bets on its cards, which grant status, night credits and a fourth award night free.

None of them is "the best" in the abstract. The right choice depends on where you travel (is there a hotel from this family near your usual destinations?), how you earn (nights versus card spend) and what you want status to do (breakfast, late checkout, upgrades or cheaper awards).

How award pricing differs

  • Hyatt is the only one of the four that still publishes an award chart with fixed point amounts: eight categories, each with five redemption levels since May 2026.
  • Hilton and IHG say outright that award prices move with demand and the room rate, and can change daily.
  • Marriott does not publish a chart on its redemption pages; you see the points price when you search a date.
  • Long stays: Marriott makes the fifth night of a five-night award stay complimentary, Hilton gives Silver members and above every fifth night free on all-points Standard Room Rewards, and IHG gives its cardmembers the fourth night free on a four-night points stay.

Status from a credit card

For most people the quickest route to hotel status is not nights but a co-brand card. Each program hands out status differently: Hilton grants tiers outright, Marriott and IHG add elite night credits, and Hyatt adds qualifying night credits on top of entry status.

Transferable points

Chase Ultimate Rewards lists IHG, Marriott Bonvoy and World of Hyatt as hotel transfer partners (transfers need a Sapphire Preferred, Sapphire Reserve or Ink Business Preferred, go in 1,000-point increments and are final). American Express Membership Rewards is widely reported to transfer to Hilton and Marriott; we could not load Amex’s partner page to confirm the ratios, so check them there. Transfer only when you have found the award you want.

Read this as a map, not a plan. Card status lasts only while you keep the card, annual fees add up, and spending money you would not otherwise spend to reach a threshold is never worth it.

What the comparison tells you

  • Easiest status without staying: Hilton. The Surpass card hands you Gold, with its daily food and beverage credit or breakfast, and Aspire hands you Diamond.
  • Best for predictable award prices: Hyatt. A published chart with category caps, and Chase points transfer in.
  • Most flexible points: Marriott. The biggest brand list, plus transfers out to 38 airline programs.
  • Best card perks for award stays: IHG. Cardmembers get the fourth award night free, and the cards add up to 20 elite night credits each January.
  • Breakfast is a top-tier perk almost everywhere except Hilton, where it starts at Gold.

Sources and verification

Benefits change. Hotel programs and card issuers revise tiers, award prices and card perks often, sometimes mid-year, and many elite benefits are "subject to availability" at the hotel. Everything here was checked against the program’s or issuer’s own pages on October 19, 2026. Treat it as education, not advice, and confirm the current terms before you book, apply for a card or change how you spend.

Every claim on this hub comes from a program guide, and each guide lists its sources: the program’s own pages and press releases and the card issuers’ pages. Where we could not confirm something on an official page, the guide says so in a "What we could not verify" list instead of guessing.
