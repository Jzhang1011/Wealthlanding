Privacy Policy

How WealthLanding handles information on a mostly static educational site.

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

WealthLanding (“we,” “us”) publishes educational personal-finance content and browser-based tools. This policy describes information that may be collected when you visit wealthlanding.com, subscribe to our optional newsletter, or use certain tools that store preferences in your browser.

Information we may collect

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics with measurement ID G-9JMJJ5CJR4 to understand aggregate traffic (for example, pages viewed, approximate location derived by Google, device/browser type, and referral source). Google may set cookies or similar identifiers as described in Google’s policies.
  • Newsletter. If you subscribe, you provide an email address (and any fields you choose to complete) through Kit / ConvertKit forms embedded on the site. That data is processed by Kit to send the emails you opted into. You can unsubscribe using the link in those emails.
  • Browser local storage. Some tools (for example, credit-card wallet helpers and calculators) may save preferences or non-sensitive inputs in your browser’s localStorage so your session is more convenient. This data stays on your device unless you clear site data; we do not receive a copy of those local values as part of that storage mechanism. Do not enter Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, passwords, or other highly sensitive identifiers into any form or tool on this site.
  • Communications. If you contact us through a form or newsletter-related channel, we may retain the content of your message to respond and improve the site.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies or similar technologies may be used by us or third parties (notably Google Analytics and Kit / ConvertKit) for analytics, form functionality, and remembering preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking some cookies may affect analytics or form behavior.

Third parties

We rely on service providers to operate the site, including:

  • Google (Analytics / gtag)
  • Kit (ConvertKit) for email subscriptions
  • Hosting and CDN providers that deliver static pages and assets
  • Font and icon CDNs used for styling

Those providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

How we use information

  • To operate, secure, and improve the educational site and tools
  • To measure aggregate usage and content performance
  • To send newsletter content you requested
  • To respond to messages you send us

Data retention and your choices

Analytics retention follows our Google Analytics configuration and Google’s product settings. Newsletter records are retained while you remain subscribed (and as needed for compliance or suppression lists). Local storage can be cleared anytime in your browser.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete certain personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request related to newsletter or site communications, contact us using the method below.

Children

WealthLanding is intended for a general adult audience interested in personal finance education. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, reach WealthLanding through the newsletter form in the site footer and note that your message is a privacy request. We do not publish a separate postal address on this page.
