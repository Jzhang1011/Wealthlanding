Methodology

4 Pillars of International Retirement Testing

Before committing capital or selling a home, test your target city with this structured framework.

01

90-Day Test Stay

Rent in a local neighborhood during non-peak tourist season. Test grocery shopping, pharmacies, public transit, and daily housing comfort.

02

Roth & Tax Residency

Verify local foreign income tax treaties, U.S. state domicile exit rules, foreign bank account reporting (FBAR/FATCA), and Roth conversion treatment.

03

Healthcare Coverage

Traditional Medicare generally does not cover routine care abroad. Secure international private insurance ($300–$800/mo) and emergency evacuation.

04

Visa & Capital Lockup

Confirm minimum passive income, age requirements, bank deposit rules, or real estate purchase thresholds prior to legal application.
