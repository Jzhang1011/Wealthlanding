Why Portugal Wins For Your Strategy (5-Minute Explanation) Click to expand ▼

1. D7 Visa Accessibility: Portugal's D7 visa provides legal residency based on passive retirement income (IRAs, Social Security, dividends) with a low minimum income threshold (~€820/mo per applicant).

2. World-Class JCI Healthcare: Hospitals like CUF and Luz Health offer private English-speaking care at 70% lower out-of-pocket costs than U.S. pre-Medicare insurance premiums.

3. Smooth Return Bridge: Lisbon provides 7 daily direct flights to the U.S. East Coast, making return visits to family in Raleigh or Seattle fast and accessible.
